Frequently asked questions

Which statutory compliances do you handle?

PF, ESI, Professional Tax, Labour Welfare Fund, TDS and income tax returns, along with related returns and challan payments.

Can you help with hiring as well as compliance?

Yes. Staffing is one of our four pillars, covering recruitment, talent acquisition and workforce solutions alongside statutory and strategy support.

How is pricing decided?

Pricing depends on the number of employees and the scope of services. We share a customised quotation after an initial consultation.

What information do you need to get started?

Basic company details, employee data, current payroll structure and any existing statutory registration numbers. We guide you through the rest.
